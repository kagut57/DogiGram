    Video player

    A proper player for every video people send you.

    • Subtitles from the chat

      Add .srt, .ass or .vtt files shared in the same chat, or paste a message link.

    • Subtitle style

      Any size and color, a shadow or box behind the text, and drag subtitles where you want them.

    • Subtitle and audio sync

      Shift subtitles or audio in 0.1-second steps when they drift.

    • Speed from 0.1x to 10x

      One slider, with normal speed in the middle.

    • Volume boost to 200%

      Keep swiping up past full volume for quiet videos.

    • Swipe for brightness and volume

      Left side for brightness, right side for volume.

    • Tracks and video size

      Pick audio and subtitle tracks by name, and switch between fit, fill and zoom.

    • Resume where you stopped

      Long videos continue from your last position.

    Chats and translation

    Read any language without leaving the chat.

    • Translate in place

      The message or caption itself switches to your language. No pop-up.

    • Formatting kept

      Bold text, links, hashtags and premium emoji stay where they were.

    • Back to the original

      Tap Show Original under the message. With double tap set to Translate, double-tap it again.

    • Double-tap actions

      Choose what a double tap does: react, reply, save, edit or translate.

    • Your message menu

      Pick which actions appear, including Repeat, Bookmark, Details and forwarding without the "Forwarded from" label.

    • Message details

      Everything about a message on one page, including the raw data.

    • Select a range

      Select two messages to select everything between them.

    • Search with Google Lens

      Look up any photo or video frame, even in chats that block saving.

    Reading files

    Open PDFs, comics, logs and code without another app.

    • PDF reader

      Search inside documents, follow links, and read two pages side by side in landscape.

    • Comics

      CBZ and CBR files, with right-to-left and double-page modes.

    • Text and code

      Smooth even on 10 MB logs, with line numbers, sepia and dark themes, search and go to line.

    • Edit and send back

      Change a text or code file and send the new copy to the same chat.

    • Bookmarks and collections

      Save pages and files, and pick up where you left off.

    Accounts and privacy

    Multiple accounts, and control over what others see.

    • Multiple accounts

      Log in to several accounts and switch in one tap. The account you open connects first.

    • Screenshot mode

      Hide names and phone numbers before you take screenshots.

    • Hide your number

      Keep your phone number off the side menu and settings.

    • IDs at a glance

      User, chat and data center IDs, plus an estimated account creation date.

    • Server status

      Check whether Telegram's data centers are up.

    Tools

    Small utilities that save a trip to another app.

    • Stickers to WhatsApp

      Export static and animated Telegram sticker packs to WhatsApp.

    • Files hub

      Browse downloads by type, delete in bulk, and keep original names when saving.

    • Faster transfers

      An optional boost for uploads and downloads.

    Look and feel

    Dark, violet, and the husky you'll see everywhere.

    • Dark and violet

      A dark theme with a violet accent and the DogiGram husky on the icon and notifications.

    • Classic side menu

      The original Telegram drawer, with accounts you can drag into order.

    • Small extras

      Exact counts instead of 4.8K, hide the keyboard while scrolling, pause video in the background, and more recent stickers.

    Questions

    Is DogiGram the official Telegram app?

    No. DogiGram is an independent app that connects to Telegram through its public API. Your chats stay on Telegram's servers, the same as in any Telegram app.

    Can I use my existing Telegram account?

    Yes. Sign in with your phone number and your chats, groups and channels appear as usual.

    Can I keep the official Telegram app installed?

    Yes. DogiGram installs alongside it, and you can be signed in to both.

    Does it cost anything?

    DogiGram is free. Telegram Premium features still need a Premium subscription from Telegram.

    How do I delete my data?

    See Delete your account or data for step-by-step instructions.
